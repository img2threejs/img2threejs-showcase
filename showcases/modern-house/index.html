Baked light, editable geometry

Direct sunlight and shadows are rendered in real time. Indirect bounce and reflections use the authored static bake; this is not dynamic GI.

The cutaway camera sits outside the house, so camera movement is limited to a finite viewing enclosure. It is camera safety, not physical collision.

Three ways to look
Detail 3D
Authored detail — dimensions estimated. Editable geometry and PBR materials guided by the source image. Orbit, pan and zoom inside the viewing enclosure, or jump to a room.
Viggle capture (two scenes)
Authored house (default): a Viggle capture reconstructed from an img2interior render of the authored house. It is a single-viewpoint reconstruction; unseen areas are filled in by the engine and scale is arbitrary. Original Viggle: Viggle’s capture inferred from the source image; its layout differs from the authored house and it is not registered to it. In both you look around from one fixed point; there is no walkable geometry and the room buttons only turn the view.
Source image
The source image exactly as supplied. The three views are independent; none is aligned to another pixel for pixel.

Limits
  • Metre scale, camera angle and hidden surfaces are authored estimates from one image, not survey data.
  • Indirect light and reflections come from a static bake and reflection probe. Materials and finishes do not rebake them, and mirrors are not live.
  • The Viggle capture is reconstructed from a single viewpoint. The authored-house scene follows the authored layout from that viewpoint, but unseen areas are filled in by the engine and scale is arbitrary. The original scene is an inferred panorama with its own layout; it differs from the authored house, for example by adding a bedroom window that is not in the source image, and is not registered to it.
  • Neither view sharpens or deblurs the other, and neither recovers exact 3D geometry from the source.
