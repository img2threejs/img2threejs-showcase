Reference

The showcase reference is a frame from the supplied video. The 3D house was authored from separate synthetic design references; its dimensions and hidden surfaces are estimates.

Baked light, editable geometry

Direct sunlight and shadows are rendered in real time. Indirect bounce and reflections use the authored static bake; this is not dynamic GI. The camera stays inside the declared room bounds.

Controls

Drag to orbit the camera. Scroll or pinch to zoom, right-drag to pan inside the room. Keyboard: Tab into the 3D frame, arrow keys to rotate, + / − to zoom, Home returns to the source view.

Press F to toggle fullscreen. Fullscreen is the best way to explore the house.
