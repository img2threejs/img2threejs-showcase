Behind the showcase / the img2threejs workflow

From reference.
To something you can explore.

img2threejs provides the core workflow: study the reference, assemble the model, author its behavior, and verify the result in the browser.

The core / img2threejs

The model is the starting point.
The experience is built around it.

Model assembly, a continuous folding display, camera choreography, six finishes, two screen choices, lighting and interaction come together in this showcase. Each is refined against the reference and checked in motion.

  1. 01 / Read the reference

    Start with the product story.

    Read and analyze Apple’s iPhone Duo page: the closed and open silhouettes, camera layout, White and Night finishes, screen transitions and titanium hinge. These observations define what the showcase needs to express.

  2. 02 / Build the reference library

    Save the details worth returning to.

    Collect the supplied media set of four photographs and ten films, preserve the source links, and verify image sizes and video timing. Still crops and sampled film frames become the working references for color, screen artwork and motion.

    Explore the saved references ↓

  3. 03 / Inspect and compare

    Turn observations into decisions.

    Study the body proportions, lens stack, display boundaries and hinge from multiple views. Compare candidate geometry with the reference before choosing the foundation for the interactive model.

    Optional reference study / Hyper3D MCP

    Rodin generated a five-view study during this exploration. Its screen and camera details did not match closely enough for the final model. This was an optional reference experiment within the img2threejs workflow; its geometry is not used in the final articulated model.

  4. 04 / Assemble with img2threejs

    Build the interactive foundation.

    External models may be viewed as optional visual references. Geometry can also be built procedurally from images and measurements. This showcase retains previously measured Apple-source surfaces in code, then adds hinge controls and a connected display that follows the fold. GLB files are prohibited in its source, build and runtime.

    Surface reference: Apple’s original model. Custom showcase assembly and behavior: the img2threejs workflow. Visual references and Hyper3D are optional research tools.

  5. 05 / Author the experience

    Give every detail a behavior.

    Reconstruct Display reveal and Titanium hinge from the films. Add screen blur, camera movement and local shadows; tune White and Night; introduce four custom finishes and Architecture artwork. Tie everything to playback, scrubbing and direct inspection.

  6. 06 / Verify and refine

    Check the result while it moves.

    Review 48 finish, screen and pose captures and 12 live playback runs. Check the connected screen across 55 viewing angles, keyboard controls on mobile, repeated screen changes and graphics recovery. Visible reference differences remain part of the review.

    Explore the finished showcase ↑
Inside the supporting Hyper3D experiment

Five consistent views of one closed device were rendered from the Apple source model and submitted through Hyper3D MCP. The resulting static Rodin study was evaluated during reference analysis. It does not contain the final folding, screen or finish behavior.
